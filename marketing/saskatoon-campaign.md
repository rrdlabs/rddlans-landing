# rrdlabs.online — Saskatoon Social Campaign

**Campaign codename:** `crush-the-599`
**Region:** Saskatoon & Saskatchewan (YXE)
**Platforms:** Instagram + Threads, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook

## Why this campaign

Competitors charge **$599+ for a landing page alone**. rrdlabs builds **fully developed,
security-hardened apps for registered charities — free**. This campaign makes that
difference undeniable, turns the volunteer build program into a lead engine, and
positions rrdlabs as **the #1 dev shop in the Saskatoon region** for anyone who needs
software done right, priced honestly.

That honesty is the moat: quotes are free, landing pages start at $100, and price only
scales upward with the *gravity of the sale*. The free charity builds prove real
capability. Price wins the deal; proof wins trust.

## Narrative pillars (the story engine)

Every post should serve one of three pillars — plus the pricing wedge.

1. **Volunteer / For Good** — free apps for charities. Proof: BCW Mobile (Bridge City
   Warmth street-outreach app), free build slots, the "no catch" policy.
2. **Developments / Now** — what we ship. Proof: Spider Protocol, BCW Mobile, CODEPET
   (ESP32-S3 AI pet), seedy/vuln research, the rrdlabs.online relaunch.
3. **Future / YXE** — where Saskatoon goes. Free charity app slots, local hiring/partners,
   community events, "we build for our own city" stance.

The **pricing wedge** threads through everything: `market does $599 for a page → we do
$100, hardened → price accumulates upward with the gravity of the sale → full apps for
charities at $0`. The site's `/pricing` section is the landing target for all CTAs.

## 4-week content calendar

Week pacing: **Mon/Wed/Fri = creative posts**, **Tue/Thu = proof/developments**, **Sat = community/volunteer**, Sun = rest or comments-only.

### Week 1 — The Receipt (price wedge + brand buffer)
1. M: Launch post across all platforms: "Market charges $599 for a page. We ship landing pages from $100, full charity apps at $0 — free quotes, no strings. New pricing is live on rrdlabs.online." → link to `/#pricing`.
2. T (X/LinkedIn/TikTok): The Receipt — competitor quote screenshot vs our pricing breakdown. Punchline: "You're not paying for a page. You're paying for a monopoly."
3. W (IG/FB): Carousel: "What $599 buys elsewhere" vs "What $100 buys here" vs "What charities pay: $0."
4. T (X): Terminal-style screenshot post announcing the site relaunch as rrdlabs.online.
5. F (all): "Build #1 or 0 — the story." Intro post for charity free-build program + application link.
6. S (FB): Post in 2–3 YXE local/volunteer groups with the free-build offer (no hard sell).

### Week 2 — Proving It (volunteer work, BCW)
1. M: BCW Mobile case thread (X) + Reel (IG/TikTok): offline-first donation & dispatch app for a street-outreach crew.
2. T: LinkedIn long-form: "We built a charity's entire operation into one app. Free. Here's why + the business case for doing it again."
3. W: Reel: 60-second app walkthrough — donate → claim pickup → help request → warm meal. End card: "#NoCatchEver — free for registered charities."
4. T (X): Dev-notes thread: 223 KB bundle, zero app-store wait, Stripe + e-Transfer + pledges in one screen.
5. F (all): Testimonial-style post *as the charity's voice* (with permission): "Our outreach crew runs on this now."
6. S (FB/IG): Behind-the-scenes of a volunteer build session (photos, desk, "this is the free build workshop").

### Week 3 — The Radar (developments: Spider, CODEPET, research)
1. M (all): Spider Protocol — decentralized data-removal platform explainer (animated node graphic).
2. T (X): Seedy / vuln-research tools shipped publicly — "we break stuff so your app doesn't." 
3. W (IG/TikTok): CODEPET on the Waveshare ESP32-S3 Touch AMOLED — short, mesmerizing flash demo.
4. T (LinkedIn): "Skilled volunteering pays for itself" — metric post: free build slots vs. agency invoices; businesses/boards can sponsor a home-slot.
5. F (all): The stack flex marquee-style: PWA, Stripe, Supabase, ESP32, Solidity — "this is what an honest studio fields."
6. S: Community post: "We're Saskatoon's dev shop. These are the tools we're learning next for the region."

### Week 4 — The Goal (future of YXE + conversion push)
1. M: Manifesto post: "Number #1 for the right reason" — affordable local software + free builds = thriving nonprofits + real talent in Saskatoon.
2. T (LinkedIn): Agenda for the next 12 months in the region (free slots Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4, local hiring, demo days) — aims to recruit sponsors & partner orgs.
3. W (all): "One week, one free build slot left this quarter" + application CTA (scarcity from capacity, not sales pressure).
4. T (X): The final wedge: "We've been asked why we undercut the market. Because a $599 landing page and a $0 charity app don't live in the same world to us." 
5. F (all): Recap carousel/reel: Week 1 → Week 4 — the program, the numbers, the names (with permission), what's next.
6. S: Local gratitude post tagging community partners; announce next-quarter slot intake.

## Measurement & KPIs

- **North star:** free-build applications submitted (track via mailto/contact form).
- **Weekly:** followers, reach, saves/shares, comments on proof posts, link clicks to
  `/#pricing` (UTM per platform).
- **Qualitative:** inbound "this is for my organization" messages, sponsor interest,
  local media replies.
- **Budget (optional):** $50–150/mo geo boost on the best Week 1 & Week 2 posts to
  Saskatoon+40 km; widen to Saskatchewan after 30 days if CTR > 2%.

## Guardrails

- Never shame a named competitor; keep the "$599" comparison generic ("the market").
- Charity orgs: always get written consent before naming them or showing their work.
- Do not overpromise slot capacity; keep "limited slots, impact-ranked" honest.
- Every paid/boosted ad must link to `/#pricing` or the charity application. No exceptions.
